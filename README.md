# 🌆 ACCESS CITY

### An Interactive Game for Learning Inclusive Digital Design

> **What if the interface wasn't designed for you?**

Access City is an interactive narrative game designed to help developers, UI/UX designers, product designers, and students understand the real-world impact of inaccessible digital interfaces.

Instead of creating another accessibility tool for people with disabilities, Access City focuses on the **people who design and build digital products**.

The player becomes a young digital designer, experiences an intentionally inaccessible interface, enters a fictional city, meets people with different accessibility needs, and learns how better design decisions can make digital experiences more inclusive.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Most digital products are designed around an assumed "average user."

However, people interact with technology in very different ways.

Users may experience:

- Visual impairments
- Hearing impairments
- Motor difficulties
- Cognitive or reading difficulties
- Color-vision deficiencies
- Language barriers
- Low digital literacy

When accessibility is not considered during design, even simple tasks can become frustrating or impossible.

The problem is not always a lack of assistive technology.

Sometimes, the problem is the **interface itself**.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

Access City approaches accessibility from the other side.

Instead of asking:

> "How can we build another tool for users with disabilities?"

we ask:

> **"How can we teach the people building digital products to design better interfaces in the first place?"**
